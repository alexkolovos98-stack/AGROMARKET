# AGROPLAZA – Agricultural Price Comparison Platform

## Project Overview

AGROPLAZA is a personal project exploring the idea of a price comparison website designed for farmers in Greece.

The goal is to help farmers search for agricultural products, compare prices from different suppliers and find suitable purchasing options more easily.

**Project status:** Unfinished prototype – not a live commercial platform.

## Why I Started This Project

Having worked in agriculture for several years, I understand some of the challenges farmers face when looking for agricultural supplies.

I wanted to explore a platform that could make it easier to find products, compare suppliers and save time.

The idea combines my practical agricultural experience with my interest in business analysis and digital solutions.

## Main Features of the Prototype

The prototype includes:

- Agricultural product categories and listings
- Supplier and store information
- Product search with suggestions
- Search support for Greek words without accents
- Sample products and prices for demonstration

These features are part of an early prototype and are not fully developed or commercially tested.

## Project Limitations

- The platform uses sample data rather than live supplier prices.
- Price comparison functionality is incomplete.
- The prototype has not been validated with real users.
- It is not ready for commercial use.

## What I Learned

Working on AGROPLAZA helped me understand the importance of clearly defining a problem before developing a solution.

It also highlighted the value of competitor research, testing assumptions and collecting feedback from potential users.

Although the project remains unfinished, it represents my interest in combining agriculture, business thinking and technology.
